# Мастера кампании

Четыре оригинальных PNG созданы встроенным ImageGen для «Света Авалона».
Экспорт в игру выполняет `tools/assets/prepare-campaign-art.py`.

## chapter-atlas.png

> Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: one landscape atlas of eight completely separate edge-to-edge game environment paintings in a precise 2 columns by 4 rows grid, equal cells, zero gutters. Original dark Arthurian fantasy lighthouse game. No characters, monsters, UI or lettering. Each cell is a wide editorial landscape in a single art-directed painterly dark pixel-art style with textured slate, restrained fog and focal amber beacon. Row 1 left: dark cyan rocky shore, old stone pier, circular simple beacon. Row 1 right: a sand spit curling into a steel-blue bay with a fortified beacon. Row 2 left: olive thorn marsh, small raised islands and winding dry causeways, old tower reflected in water. Row 2 right: charcoal ash cliffs, ruined masonry breach, broad plateau and an ember-lit rune beacon. Row 3 left: slate wreck channel and two piers under a lighthouse, dark ship hulls with broken masts, subtle copper. Row 3 right: broken blue-purple watchtowers during an eclipse and a single tall lit tower. Row 4 left: two rocky headlands around a dark sea rift, last beacon and a peach trace of dawn; row 4 right: same shore at dawn with blue-gray sea, subdued peach sky, ships safely leaving harbor. Each cell is its own full composition, distinct silhouettes visible at thumbnail scale. No borders nor objects bridging cells. Game art only. No text, no watermarks.

Transparent background, no ground shadows extending past sprite, no lettering, no grid. Row 3: large slate armored flying carrier; lavender ghost siren with trailing fins; drowned boarding warrior with a broken spear; red-orange barrel bomb thrower on feet. Row 4: tall pale-blue mirror knight with faceted mirror; soot-purple snuffer with a cage and hood; empty cell; empty cell. Transparent actual alpha, no background objects, no humanoid faces closeup.

Crisply hand-pixelled game sprite art, selective saturated accents over readable desaturated form, viewed in three-quarter high overhead. No scenery, no text, no watermarks, no symbols outside sprites. True transparent alpha background.

## warden-atlas.png

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: a single transparent sprite strip for the original dark Arthurian coast-defense game Light of Avalon. Exactly two equal square cells in one horizontal row, one fully isolated object per cell, 10% clear padding all sides, no grid or lettering. Left: the Abyss Warden, an ancient dark-violet sea creature peering above the surface, low broad undersea cephalopod crown of mottled slate violet and two pale amber eyes, no long tentacles on this body because the separate target tips are drawn elsewhere. Right: one compact curling tentacle tip with a strong taper, dark purple silhouette, three copper-purple suckers and a muted violet ridge. Three-quarter elevated game camera, hand-pixelled readable silhouettes, muted teal and charcoal shadows, thin crisp rim highlights still readable at 35–70px. Edge-to-edge transparent alpha, no water or environment, no floor and no objects shared between cells. Output for a game art atlas.
