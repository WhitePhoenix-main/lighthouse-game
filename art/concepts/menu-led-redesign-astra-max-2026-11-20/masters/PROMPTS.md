Встроенный ImageGen получил три отдельных задания без внешних изображений. В первом и третьем задании фон непрозрачный, во втором запрошен альфа-канал. Имена ниже соответствуют сохранённым оригиналам.

## journal-plates.png

> Use case: illustration-story. Create ONE ORIGINAL portrait 2-column by 4-row full-bleed atlas for the eight contiguous copperplate woodblock illustrations of a dark maritime pixel-art game journal. 1024 wide x 1536 tall, eight equal 512x384 LANDSCAPE rectangular cells, clean straight boundaries at exactly middle x and 25/50/75 percent y, NO gutter, borders, paper page, lettering, symbols, numbers, speech bubbles, or graphic UI. Each cell a different fully composed scenic image, recognisable from silhouette, crisp clustered pixels, extremely limited 12-bit palette. Art like hand-hewn late-1990s richly textured pixel engravings printed in midnight navy, steel and slate blue, ivory and candle-flame copper amber; elegantly dramatic light and broken highlights reminiscent of a stone lighthouse at night. No antialiasing or painterly blur. Use broad readable shapes and close foreground framing. AROUND TWO THIRDS MIDVALUE rather than all black. Order row 1 left: a dripping giant sea tentacle reaches a stone pier in the lamp beam, close lantern at left. Row 1 right: the tattered tricorn hat and silhouette of a spectral drowned sea captain calling his crew along a sandbar, chilly moon. Row 2 left: a dense braided black root rises like a heart from a wet islet below an old beacon, teal marsh. Row 2 right: a rocky ash colossus above a broken crenellated wall and a scorched beacon under a copper ember sky. Row 3 left: the silhouette of a massive old ship bell hanging among tangled masts over a wreck channel, amber rimlight. Row 3 right: a hooded fallen lighthouse keeper in silhouette raising a deceptive signal lamp surrounded by ruined towers and eclipsed sun. Row 4 left: a swirling cut of light in the sea fog around a luminous irregular heart above a deep ocean rift, high drama, purple blue and pale copper. Row 4 right: hopeful sunrise over a distant chain of SEVEN coastal beacon silhouettes and a small sailing ship returning home, bright pearl sky and rich copper light reflecting in the waves. All feel from one deeply crafted hand-carved pixel-illustrated travel book. Edge-to-edge scenic compositions, no text of any kind.

## watch-sprites.png

> Use case: stylized-concept. One game-ready transparent sprite ATLAS asset in a strict aligned four-column by two-row grid, eight completely separated individual objects with generous transparent margins around EVERY object. A landscape 1536x1024 image. Each object centered inside its equal cell. No panel backgrounds, no grid lines, no text, no lettering, no ground plane or stage, no artificial outer drop shadow. Dense but clean handcrafted pixel art, true classic bitmapped fantasy strategy art at 48px entity scale; flat small clusters with limited colors, sharp black-navy keylines, pale sandstone highlights from upper left and cold navy shadows. Camera overhead three-quarter (isometric 30 degrees from above). Palette midnight blue gray, muted teal, old brass, copper. Each base is isolated and recognizable at thumbnail size. Top row left to right: (1) a single unique sturdy tapered ROUND medieval masonry lighthouse watchtower with blue slate masonry blocks, subtle ivy, glowing orange glass lantern room, dark little dome on top, warm door at base; solitary small tower, no island. (2) a squat bronze-barrel naval cannon on a blue-gray wheeled stone turret base, pointing left; (3) a small arcane light trap, brass circular floor plate with three wedge prongs guarding a pale cyan crystal in its center, very low; (4) a single menacing thick dark sea-monster tentacle curling upward like a question mark, visible orange-ivory suckers, cropped cleanly at its base. Bottom row left to right: (1) a tattered spectral ghost drifting horizontally in the air, hooded face and cool pale blue teal rags; (2) a bulky massive armored sea turtle looking toward the right, faceted ridged slate shell and thick feet; (3) a dangerous charging minotaur seen in three quarter profile facing right, bronze horns and dark ruddy armor, powerful stance and lowered head; (4) a bat in flight front three-quarter with distinctive pointed wings and moonlit violet membrane. No ambiguity between grid cells. Amber lamp, threats clear, strong well-defined silhouettes. No human protagonist. Genuinely TRANSPARENT background.

## coastal-materials.png

> Use case: stylized-concept. Create one square texture-atlas master of four original seamless pixel art terrain MATERIAL SWATCHES in an exact TWO-by-TWO grid. Four full-bleed square swatches sharing exact horizontal and vertical middle lines. 1024 x 1024. It is a game developer asset, NOT a screenshot. No text, no numbers, no stylized borders, no long poles, no buildings, no landmark, no people, no UI. For 1990s premium fantasy isometric maritime strategy pixel art, northern coast at night; rich carefully selected navy/slate/steel blue with small patches of dull gold/teal. Sharp pixel clusters. Each square is top-down orthographic with simulated light coming from the upper left. Upper-left tile: a rough coastal heath of closely packed small slate terraces, creeping moss and severed old cobble stones with short scattered grasses. Ground feels organically tessellated and muted, mostly dark desaturated slate blue with isolated ochre moss. Upper-right tile: uninterrupted deep NAVY nighttime open water with clusters of horizontally stretched, small irregular quiet wave crests catching steel blue light, subtle varied broad ripples, very low contrast, no shores or rocks. Lower-left tile: face of a broken basalt cliff, thin angular VERTICAL striations and deep joints, blue-black and cool steel glints across rugged column faces seen near straight on. Lower-right tile: a drier weather-worn ancient flagstone pathway, thin irregular cracks, deep blue-gray cobbles with sparse tarnished amber highlights, horizontal/diagonal fractured tablets. No distinct scene or horizon, each quadrant must be flat tileable texture only, similar native material detail scale 3–6 pixels per cluster. Restrained, richly textured, cool nocturnal clarity; limited palette.
